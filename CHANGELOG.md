# Changelog

## [3.0.0] — Portal FLASH Edition (2026-10-29)

### Added
- Full-screen beat-reactive neon color flashes during all nine Portal Runs.
- Animated aurora strips, shimmering skyline windows and moving stars.
- Giant pulsing portal sun and multiple concentric energy rings.
- Enhanced glowing obstacles, animated runway reflections and cube trails.
- A luminous multi-layer finish gate and more dynamic progress highlights.
- New visual effects do not consume HP or change collision geometry.

### Preserved
- 100% solo mode, choice between Blue and Metal, four difficulty settings.
- Ten combat waves and nine cube transitions.
- One available special ability is lost on portal collision; no HP loss.
- Unlimited retry attempts and offline single-file browser play.

### Note
This is the PC/browser release; no new Android APK is included. Effects may flash or pulse and may be uncomfortable for photosensitive players.
