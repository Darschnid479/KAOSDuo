# KAOSDUO CHANGELOG

## v2.0.0 — OVERDRIVE (2026-10-29)

### Massive content upgrade
- Expanded the campaign from 7 to **10 waves**.
- Added **3 unique boss encounters** at waves 4, 7, and 10, each with enraged second-phase attacks.
- Added **Charger**, **Sentinel**, and **Crystal** enemy archetypes, plus unique visuals, charge attacks, and projectiles.
- Added telegraphed meteors and glitch area hazards (warning circles before impact).
- Added a **double-jump** and a damage-dealing, temporarily invulnerable **dash** (Shift or Q).
- Added an **OVERDRIVE** meter that fills from kills and powerups, enabling faster, stronger attacks and speed for nine seconds.
- Expanded from 10 to **17 upgrade types**, adding crit chance, life-on-kill, chain lightning, dash cooldown, orbital turret, blast damage, and faster rage generation.
- Updated screen effects, damage numbers, projectile trails, backgrounds, boss bars, audio beats, HUD, and menu.
- Added touch dash button and kept the **single-player-only** rule.
- Packaged an all-in-one **offline HTML game** with embedded pixel art.

### Known limitations
- Browser and offline HTML release. Android native APK is not included.
- Web hosting through GitHub Pages requires Pages to be enabled in repository settings.
- Keyboard controls are best suited for desktop; touch experience requires landscape mode.

## v1.x — SOLO EDITION
