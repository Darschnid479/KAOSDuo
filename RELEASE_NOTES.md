# KAOSDUO: OVERDRIVE — v2.0.0

**The biggest KAOSDUO update yet.** One fighter. Ten waves. Three bosses. Zero mercy.

## Highlights
- 🔥 **OVERDRIVE RAGE MODE** — build power by fighting and become a wrecking ball.
- 👑 **3 bosses** — Glitch Warden, Crystal Titan and Portal King, with two phases each.
- 👾 **3 new enemy types** — Charger, Sentinel and Crystal.
- 🚀 **Double jump + turbo dash** — mobility and evasion completely reworked.
- ⚡ **17 upgrade choices** — chain lightning, crits, orbital turret, lifesteal and more.
- ☄️ **Arena hazards** — telegraphed meteor and glitch strike zones.
- 🎮 **Solo only** — choose Blue or Metal. All four difficulties remain.
- 🎵 **New audio layers and VFX**, particles, combo/rage HUD, boss bars and upgraded menu.

### Controls
A/D or arrows = move, W/Up/Space = double jump, Shift/Q = dash, F/K = fire, G/L = super, P = pause, M = sound.
