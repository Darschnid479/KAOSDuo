# 💥 KAOSDUO: PORTAL AMBUSH v4.2.0

**The portal can turn against you at any moment.**

### The surprise shooting ambush
Each of the nine portals has an independent **25% chance** for an unannounced miniboss encounter. It triggers *during* the level, not at the finish line. The screen transforms into a shooting encounter: fly, dodge enemy fire and defeat the Portal Sentinel with F or X. The run resumes from the same point once the boss is defeated. A retry does not reroll the miniboss.

### Spend your coins between waves
Monster kills award spendable coins. After every finished combat wave, a new dialog asks: **Would you like to buy a new weapon?** Choose Yes to enter the 8-weapon Armory, or No to continue directly into the portal. The shop pauses gameplay and purchased weapons are included in saves.

### More variation
Portal courses alternate flight/gravity sequences across levels, with neon pulse effects and the existing cube-jump opening. Four difficulties, two original character soundtracks, all weapons, and Save/Load remain. Portal failures cost an available ability but never health.

**Downloads:** self-contained HTML (offline), or ZIP with README and guide.
