01 / THE GAME

SMALL GAME.
BIG PROBLEMS.

KAOSDUO is a frantic 2D pixel-art action game built around a single question: how long can you keep the portal monsters away?

01👾

TEN WAVES

Fight through ten brutal waves and three multi-phase bosses, with portal journeys between rounds.

02💥

FIVE CHAOS WEAPONS

Scattergun, rail cannon, splash rockets and lightning arcs. Switch freely with 1–5!

03⭐

LEVEL UP

Collect 17 upgrades in the arena, but watch out: crashing in a Portal Run can disable a special ability.

04🏆

BEAT YOUR BEST

Beat your personal best across four difficulties and survive the Portal King.

02 / NEW IN v4.0 · FLY · FLIP · FIGHT

SURVIVE THE WAVE.
ESCAPE THE GEOMETRY.

After every fight, run, FLY, flip gravity, and sometimes duel a glowing mini boss! Your hero becomes a cube, a flying ship, then a gravity-defying acrobat. Finish all three phases to progress.

◆ 9 PORTAL MISSIONS

THREE MODES.
ONE WAY OUT.

Jump, fly through laser gates, reverse gravity and fight a surprise mini-boss in the neon portal dimension. Tap to jump, hold to fly, flip gravity, and shoot with F/X against the miniboss. Crashing never costs HP, but destroys one remaining special ability for the rest of your run.

🚀 Dash⬆ Double jump⚡ Super attack🔥 OVERDRIVE

Unlimited retries. Keep fighting when you finish. All nine routes are solvable without special powers.
