01 / THE GAME

SMALL GAME.
BIG PROBLEMS.

KAOSDUO is a frantic 2D pixel-art action game built around a single question: how long can you keep the portal monsters away?

01👾

TEN WAVES

Fight through ten brutal waves and three multi-phase bosses, with portal journeys between rounds.

02💥

WILD ABILITIES

Freeze the chaos as Blue, go full Metal with shockwaves, and unleash PORTAL RUN rage mode.

03⭐

LEVEL UP

Collect 17 upgrades in the arena, but watch out: crashing in a Portal Run can disable a special ability.

04🏆

BEAT YOUR BEST

Beat your personal best across four difficulties and survive the Portal King.

02 / NEW IN v2.1

SURVIVE THE WAVE.
ESCAPE THE GEOMETRY.

Every cleared combat wave opens a neon obstacle course. Your hero becomes a cube. You must reach the finish line before the next wave unlocks.

◆ 9 UNIQUE PORTAL RUNS

ONE BUTTON.
REAL CONSEQUENCES.

Auto-run through glowing spikes, spinning saws and cube traps. Tap, press Space, W or ↑ to jump. Crashing never costs HP, but destroys one remaining special ability for the rest of your run.

🚀 Dash⬆ Double jump⚡ Super attack🔥 OVERDRIVE

Unlimited retries. Keep fighting when you finish. All nine routes are solvable without special powers.
